## Description (English)
Sword of the Word helps you memorize Scripture and keep it for life.

Pick a verse or a whole passage, choose your start date and your pace, and the app builds your calendar. Each new verse gets focused repetition over its first four days, then a short daily review, then weekly, then monthly, so what you learn stays with you.

• Your pace: a new verse every day, every few days, weekly, or on the weekdays you choose
• Your calendar: see every day's verses at a glance
• Gentle reminders at the time you pick for each day of the week, with a snooze you set yourself
• Hide the words to test yourself
• Grace mode: fell behind? Pick up where you left off, no guilt
• Groups: start a group, share a 6-letter code, and see who has finished today's verses
• King James Version (English) and Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish) built in, or paste your own verses for personal use
• English and Spanish
• Free. No ads. No account.

The Word of God is your Sword. Keep it sharp in your heart for life's daily battles.

## Notes for the Apple reviewer (App Review Information)
No sign-in is needed. On first open, type any first name, pick a passage, and tap through the 5 short setup steps. To test groups: open the Group tab, type a group name, tap "Choose the passage and schedule", finish the steps, and a 6-letter code appears. Bible text is the public-domain King James Version and Reina-Valera 1909. The sword in the name and icon is the biblical image of Scripture as "the sword of the Spirit" (Ephesians 6:17); the app has no violent content. Donations, if ever offered, are for a ministry and are not tied to any app feature.
