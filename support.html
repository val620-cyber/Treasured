Sword of the Word: Support

Sword of the Word is a free app for memorizing Scripture. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Contact

Send a question, report a problem, or report a group name or nickname that should be removed: open a support request. Reported names are reviewed and removed when they are inappropriate.

Common questions

How do I add a passage? Tap Personal, then "Add a verse or passage". Choose the King James Version (Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish) or paste verses from your own Bible.

How does the schedule work? A new verse is repeated 25, 20, 15 and 10 times over its first four days, then once a day for 45 days, once a week for 7 weeks, and then once a month.

I fell behind. What now? Open Personal and tap "Grace mode". Your schedule continues from today.

How do groups work? Tap Group, name your group and choose a passage. Share the 6-letter code. Friends tap Group, enter the code and tap Join.

I am not getting reminders. On iPhone, open the Settings app, tap Notifications, find Sword of the Word and allow notifications. Reminder times are set inside the app under Settings.

How do I delete my information? In the app, open Settings, go to About, and tap "Delete my group data". Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no purchases, no sign-up.
