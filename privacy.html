Sword of the Word: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Sword of the Word is a free Scripture memorization app. It has no ads, no tracking, no purchases and no sign-up. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your first name or nickname, your passages, your progress, your reminder times and your settings are saved on your own device. Reminders are created by your device. We cannot see any of this.

What is stored online, only if you use groups

Groups are optional. If you start or join a group, the app stores these things in Google Firebase so the people in your group can see each other's progress:

  • your first name or nickname
  • the group's name, passage and schedule
  • how many repetitions you finished each day (your progress counts)
  • a random anonymous ID that the app creates for your device. It is not your Apple ID, phone number, email or advertising ID.

Anyone who has a group's 6-letter code can see that group's nicknames and progress. When the app talks to Firebase or to the Bible text service, those servers receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, as with any internet connection. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

Bible text

All three Bibles in the app (the American Standard Version of 1901, the King James Version and the Spanish Reina-Valera 1909, all public domain) are built into the app. Looking up verses works without internet and sends nothing anywhere, with one exception: 48 of the 1,189 chapters of the Spanish Reina-Valera 1909 are looked up through a public Bible text service (bible-api.com). Only the passage reference is sent.

Deleting your information

In the app, open Settings, go to About, and tap "Delete my group data". This removes your nickname, your progress counts, any groups you started and your anonymous ID from the server. Leaving a group removes your information from that group. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes everything saved on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A nickname is all that is ever asked for.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: email hebrews412apphelp@gmail.com or see the support page.

Espada de la Palabra: Política de privacidad

Espada de la Palabra (Sword of the Word) es una aplicación gratuita para memorizar las Escrituras. No tiene anuncios, rastreo, compras ni registro. No vendemos tu información ni la compartimos con fines publicitarios.

En tu dispositivo: tu nombre o apodo, tus pasajes, tu progreso, tus horas de recordatorio y tus ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo.

En línea, solo si usas grupos (opcional): la aplicación guarda en Google Firebase tu nombre o apodo, el nombre, pasaje y calendario del grupo, cuántas repeticiones terminaste cada día y un identificador anónimo al azar. Cualquier persona con el código del grupo puede ver los apodos y el progreso de ese grupo. Los servidores reciben información técnica normal, como tu dirección IP. No recopilamos tu correo, teléfono, ubicación ni contactos.

Texto bíblico: las tres Biblias (American Standard Version 1901, King James y Reina-Valera 1909, todas de dominio público) vienen dentro de la aplicación. Buscar versículos funciona sin internet y no envía nada, con una excepción: 48 de los 1.189 capítulos de la Reina-Valera 1909 se consultan en un servicio público (bible-api.com). Solo se envía la referencia.

Borrar tu información: en la aplicación, abre Ajustes, ve a Acerca de y toca "Borrar mis datos de grupo". Al borrar la aplicación se elimina todo lo guardado en tu dispositivo.

Contacto: escribe a hebrews412apphelp@gmail.com o consulta la página de ayuda.
